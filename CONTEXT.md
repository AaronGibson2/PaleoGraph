# PaleoGraph

PaleoGraph distinguishes retained scientific assertions from the interpretations used to browse them.

## Language

**Source taxon concept**: A provider-scoped classification or identification with its own identity, wording and supporting evidence. Equal names do not establish concept identity.

**Taxonomy browse node**: A reversible display grouping of compatible source classifications in represented evidence. Its classification relationships are not evolutionary ancestry.
_Avoid_: canonical taxon, ancestor species

**Browse placement**: A versioned interpretation placing a source classification in the explorer, preserving explicit path disagreements and unresolved membership.

**Evidence record**: A represented museum material assertion or published occurrence assertion, with its source identity retained. It is not a count of individual organisms or physical pieces.

**Direct evidence**: Evidence classified at a browse node itself.

**Inclusive evidence**: Evidence classified at a browse node or any of its displayed descendants, counted once per source assertion.

**Observed temporal envelope**: The outer bounds of represented evidence with known ages, retaining the distinction between source numeric bounds and interpreted reference envelopes. It is not biological duration.
